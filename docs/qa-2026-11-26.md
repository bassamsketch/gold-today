# Full QA, 26 November 2026

The owner asked for a full independent QA of every page, element, figure and button, and for the Buy & sell page to be rated as it stands before any further change. Four independent testers (Today with the header, My gold, Outlook, Buy & sell) drove the app in their own headless browsers at 360, 390 and 430 px, in Arabic and English, in rials, dirhams, euros and pounds. They recomputed every figure from the formulas and data, about 700 values. A fifth reviewer rated Buy & sell for the two older users. Afterwards the fixes were checked by script in the browser, and by eye at 360 and 390 px.

Severity: critical means a wrong figure or a broken function. Important means misleading text or a layout break. Minor means polish.

Today's fixes remove the defects behind several of these scores. They clear the example as soon as a box is tapped, keep a typed price when the rate route opens, show figures in whole rials in both checks, place the stale-price line under the figure it qualifies, and replace the advice wording. The design changes that would lift the tab further are U1 to U8 below. None of them is built, as the owner asked to see the rating first.

### Recommendations for Buy & sell

The owner chose U1 to U4, which were built the same day (cache v10). For uniformity, "jewellery or a bar?" also took its moment into the title ("للادخار: مشغولات أم سبيكة؟") when the red lines went. U5 to U8 are not built.

- U1. In B1, put the verdict first in bold, then three rows: gold value, the shop's extra, what a shop pays back. Fold the range, the basis and "82 of every 100" under "Show details", and remove the bar with the dot, which looks like a slider. About ten fewer lines. Small.
- U2. Put the moment in the titles ("قبل الشراء: هل السعر معتاد؟", "قبل البيع: هل العرض معتاد؟") and drop the red lines above them, so the two checks cannot be mixed up. Small.
- U3. Say B2's result in money: "قيمة الذهب اليوم نحو 809 ريالات، والعرض أقل منها بـ 29 ريالاً", instead of the percent line. Small.
- U4. Remove the break-even line ("ليعود مبلغ 950 ريالاً كاملاً..."). It reads like a price target. Small.
- U5. Cut B3's fine print to one line ("تقديرات، والأسعار تختلف من محل إلى آخر"). Small.
- U6. Larger text for older eyes: 18 px body and 16 px labels, on this tab or across the app. The installed app has no zoom, and the iPhone's Larger Text setting does not reach it. Medium.
- U7. Drop the "نصائح" heading, merge the receipt and second-shop tips into B2, and fold Places away. Small.
- U8. Fit field 3's label on one line, and drop the photo and the repeated heading, so all three boxes fit on the first screen. Small.

## Findings and what was done

### Today and the header (TD)
- TD1 critical, fixed: at the weekend with the feed down, a Wednesday price was labelled "this is Friday evening's last price". A weekend price is now called Friday's only if it is from Friday's close, and otherwise it shows its own time.
- TD2 important, fixed: in winter, gold trades until 22:00 UTC (17:00 New York), but the app ended the day at 21:00 UTC. The weekend change line measured only Friday's last hour. The market's hours now follow New York time all year. Closes are still recorded at 21:07 UTC, so the daily job and the news routine are unchanged.
- TD3 important, fixed: Today and My gold used the saved price, while Outlook, Buy & sell and the currency sheet used the newer of the saved price and the last close. Every tab now uses the same price.
- TD4 important, fixed. This was a regression from the percentage fix earlier the same day: a news file holding something other than text stopped the whole app. Such a file now hides the news card, one tab's error no longer stops the others, and a test rejects such a file before it can be committed.
- TD5 important, fixed: a change line against a close more than six days old named a weekday of an earlier week. It now names the date.
- TD6 important, fixed: the English sheet caption ended "in each". It now ends "in each currency".
- TD7 minor, fixed: Today now reopens at its top.
- TD9 minor, fixed: "10 جرام" became "10 جرامات".
- TD11 minor, fixed: the first-visit captions said "last price" while loading and "now" next to "not available".
- TD12 minor, fixed: under 380 px the currency button keeps a small chevron.
- TD13 minor, fixed: the status line's hidden separator follows the language.
- TD8 minor, deferred: a saved euro or pound choice shows rials for a moment at start-up.
- TD10 minor, deferred: in pounds the change line reads "0.069 مليون ليرة".

### My gold (MG)
- MG1 critical, fixed: an old saved price (for example from 10 days ago) gave a value 12% off with no date. The tab now uses the shared price and names its time when it is over 15 minutes old.
- MG2 important, fixed: today's value could fall below "the lowest in 12 months". Today now counts in the range.
- MG3 important, fixed: a 24 karat piece was valued with the 2-6% jewellery deduction (424 rials) while the price check used about 1% for a bar (437). 24 karat now counts as a bar or coin everywhere, as the karat guide says.
- MG4 important, fixed: in euros and pounds, past amounts (such as 349.2 million pounds paid in 2019) use today's exchange rate. A line now says so.
- MG5 important, fixed: a long name without spaces pushed the page wider at 360 px.
- MG6 important, fixed: the add sheet hid Save below the screen and opened the keyboard at once. Save and Cancel now stay on screen, and the keyboard waits for a tap.
- MG7 minor, fixed: the price paid is now 1 to 1,000,000 rials.
- MG8 minor, fixed: weights and prices show as typed, up to three decimals, including in the shared list, which is the only backup.
- MG9 minor, fixed: a month without a year, or a month after this one, now shows a message.
- MG10 minor, fixed: the headline unit and the gram counts now agree with their numbers.
- MG11 minor: the 12-month dates now carry their year (fixed). "My gold before" values the same pieces at past prices even if they were bought later, as designed.
- MG12 minor, fixed: a loss under half a rial now reads "no change so far".

### Outlook (OL)
- OL1 critical, fixed: the past week and month compared with a close made after the 7 and 30 days mark, so they read 1% and 12%. They now read 4% and 11%.
- OL2 important, fixed: the current fall dropped out of the falls line once the price was back within 20% of the record. It now stays until the record is regained.
- OL3 important, deferred (owner decision): before 2021 the falls and the worst 10 years use World Bank monthly averages, which smooth peaks. The 1980 fall shows 62%, while by the daily fixes it was about 70%. Fixing this needs a month-end price series since 1971.
- OL4 important, fixed: within 1% of the record the page said "at its highest" beside a higher record. It now says "very close to its highest".
- OL5 important, fixed: a rising line crossed the chart's Today label. The label now sits on the side the line does not come from.
- OL6 important, fixed: without the history file the tab showed empty parts and a stray dot. It now shows one plain message.
- OL7 important, fixed: bank rows were bare numbers, and "+47%" read as a promised gain. The caption now names the date and unit, and the percentages are gone.
- OL8 important, fixed: "Usually 222% higher" read as a rule. The rows now say what happened: "Half the time it ended 6% higher or more. At worst 36% lower."
- OL9 minor, fixed: the chart's words and values were under 14 px at 360 px.
- OL10 minor, deferred: at 360 px a number can wrap away from its unit.
- OL11 minor, fixed: the forecast misses now name the London Bullion Market Association survey they come from.
- OL12 minor: the sources sheet now follows the list's order (fixed). The Goldman item, credited to Investing.com with a Yahoo Finance link, is probably a syndicated article and is left as is.
- OL13 minor: an open fold now says "Hide details", and the English reads "has fallen" (fixed). The Arabic tab name "التوقعات" sits above "هذا ليس توقعاً". Renaming it is the owner's choice.
- OL14 minor, fixed with TD3.

### Buy & sell (BS, and the reviewer's BR)
- BS1 critical, fixed: with the rate route open, any change to the weight, karat or type replaced the price typed in box 3. The route now fills box 3 only after something is typed in the route.
- BS2 important, fixed: after a language switch, the karat underline marked the wrong karat.
- BS3 important, fixed: "lower than usual" appeared beside "6.0% below", while the usual band is 2 to 6%. The verdict is now judged on the printed figure.
- BS4 important, fixed: the route's VAT and bar notes, and the market-rate hint, went stale until a keystroke.
- BS5 important, fixed: "950,500" was read as 950,500 rials. A comma with three digits after it could be thousands or baisa, so it is no longer guessed. The message asks for a dot, as in 950.500. The Arabic comma is read like a comma.
- BS6 important, fixed: typing zero got "use numbers". The messages now say the amount must be above zero.
- BS7 minor, fixed: 0.05 g was accepted although the message says from 0.1, and offers had no upper limit.
- BS8 minor, fixed: an error in the route left the old result on screen.
- BS9 minor, fixed: the example now closes the rate route, and Clear resets the making mode.
- BS10 minor, fixed: there was a double line between the places and the karat guide.
- BS11 and BR5 minor, fixed: "يُستحسن سؤال محل آخر" was advice. The low verdict now states the usual 2 to 6%. "The stamp proves the karat" became "shows".
- BS12 minor, fixed: the stale-price line now sits under the gold figure and says the figures use that price.
- BR1, fixed: example numbers stayed in the boxes, so digits typed later were added to them. Tapping any box while the example shows now empties the boxes first.
- BR2, fixed: a hidden box in the closed route kept "Clear" showing.
- BR3, fixed: the offer check now names an old price as the price check does.
- BR4, fixed: the offer check showed 809.136 where the price check shows 809. Both now use whole rials, and both prompts say "result".
- BR6, fixed: "بدلاً من ذلك؟" became "هل أعطى المحل سعر الجرام والمصنعية فقط؟", and "قطعة مشغولة" became "قطعة بتصميم دقيق".
- BR7 and BR8 are recommendations U1 and U6 above.

## During the QA run
The QA's own browser helper did not close headless Edge on Windows. Between about 19:35 and 20:06 local time, about 90 leftover browsers and their profiles (70 MB each) filled drive C to 0 MB. Claude stopped those browsers and deleted their profiles. These were only folders the helper had created that afternoon. Then the helper was fixed, and the testers re-ran every check after 20:06. Drive C had about 6.4 GB free afterwards. Nothing in the app or its data was affected.
