# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
The owner of this project, their mother and their aunt, living in Muscat, Oman. The two older women are not technical. They own gold jewellery (mostly 21 and 22 karat), sometimes buy more, and sometimes sell. They will only use the app on their phones, often in bright daylight at home or in the souq, and read Arabic first. English is a toggle for the owner.

## Product Purpose
A phone web app that answers, at a glance and without jargon:
- What is gold worth right now, per gram, in Omani rials, for each karat (live, updating every few seconds), and worldwide.
- What would my own gold fetch if I sold it today.
- Is now a cheap or expensive moment compared with this year (plain words, no analysis).
- Where in Muscat to buy or sell.
- What trusted forecasters expect in 1, 5 and 10 years, honestly labelled.
Success: they open it, understand it in five seconds, and trust it.

## Brand Personality
Precious, calm, trustworthy. It should feel like being served in a fine jewellery house: bright, unhurried, beautifully lit, with gold that catches the light. References named by the owner: Cartier and Tiffany websites. The owner wants bright screens and wants to judge the feel by seeing options.

## Anti-references
- The first three rejected mockups: dark green velvet with gradient gold text and coin buttons, a teal and white Omani arch filled with yellow liquid, and maroon story cards.
- Trading apps and gold-rate websites full of tables, statistics, candlesticks and analysis.
- Generic card stacks, pills everywhere, and template app layouts.

## Design Principles
- One clear number first. Everything else is secondary and quieter.
- Plain words instead of statistics. Say "cheaper than almost any day this year", never "RSI" or "52-week percentile".
- Gold is the hero. Real imagery or real rendered metal, never flat yellow.
- Live means visibly alive: prices tick and roll, light moves, but nothing flashes or rushes.
- Honest about uncertainty. Forecasts show who said it and are labelled as expectations.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Older users: body text at least 16px, key numbers very large, contrast at least 4.5:1 (7:1 preferred for body), tap targets at least 48px, Arabic right-to-left by default with an English toggle, reduced-motion alternatives for every animation.
